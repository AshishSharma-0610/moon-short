A twenty-five-year-old NASA intern snuck into a secure vault, stole twenty-one million dollars worth of Apollo moon rocks, and scattered them across a hotel bed just so he could tell a girl they had sex on the moon.
Thad Roberts wanted to pull off the ultimate romantic gesture. Therefore, he used his intern clearance late at night to bypass security at Johnson Space Center. But the moon rocks were locked inside a six-hundred-pound steel safe. Therefore, he loaded the entire safe onto a dolly, wheeled it past security into a getaway car, and drove straight to a nearby hotel.
You see, these weren't just rocks—they were pristine Apollo eleven and seventeen samples that scientists had spent thirty years preserving in nitrogen chambers. To get to them, Thad smashed the sealed containers open with a hammer, instantly contaminating three billion years of lunar history with bedsheets, dust, and sweat.
Desperate for cash, he posted a listing online to sell the stolen moon dust to a Belgian mineral collector. But the collector immediately called the FBI. Undercover agents set up a fake sting in Florida, met Thad at a restaurant, and slapped him in handcuffs before a single dollar changed hands.
He served six years in federal prison... making it the most expensive date in human history.
